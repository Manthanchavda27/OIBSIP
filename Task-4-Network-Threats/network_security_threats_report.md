# Research Report on Common Network Security Threats

## Introduction

Network security threats are malicious activities that target computer networks, systems, and data. These attacks can compromise confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information. Understanding common threats helps organizations improve their cybersecurity defenses and reduce risks.

---

# 1. Denial of Service (DoS) Attack

## What is a DoS Attack?

A Denial of Service (DoS) attack is a cyberattack in which an attacker floods a target system, server, or network with excessive traffic or requests, making it unavailable to legitimate users.

---

## How It Works

The attacker sends a massive number of requests to overwhelm system resources such as:
- CPU
- Memory
- Bandwidth

As a result, the server becomes slow or completely unavailable.

---

## Impact of DoS Attacks

- Website downtime
- Financial loss
- Loss of customer trust
- Business interruption

---

## Real-World Example

In 2016, the Dyn DNS attack disrupted major websites like Twitter, Netflix, and Reddit using a massive Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attack powered by infected IoT devices.

---

## Prevention Methods

- Firewalls
- Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS)
- Rate limiting
- Traffic filtering
- Load balancing

---

# 2. Man-in-the-Middle (MITM) Attack

## What is a MITM Attack?

A Man-in-the-Middle attack occurs when an attacker secretly intercepts communication between two parties without their knowledge.

---

## How It Works

The attacker positions themselves between the sender and receiver and captures or modifies transmitted data.

Common targets include:
- Public Wi-Fi users
- Banking sessions
- Login credentials

---

## Impact of MITM Attacks

- Data theft
- Credential compromise
- Financial fraud
- Session hijacking

---

## Real-World Example

Attackers on unsecured public Wi-Fi networks often intercept user traffic to steal passwords and personal information.

---

## Prevention Methods

- HTTPS encryption
- VPN usage
- Secure Wi-Fi
- Multi-factor authentication
- SSL/TLS certificates

---

# 3. Spoofing Attack

## What is Spoofing?

Spoofing is a cyberattack where an attacker disguises themselves as a trusted source to deceive users or systems.

---

## Types of Spoofing

- IP Spoofing
- Email Spoofing
- ARP Spoofing
- DNS Spoofing

---

## Impact of Spoofing

- Unauthorized access
- Malware distribution
- Data theft
- Identity impersonation

---

## Real-World Example

Phishing emails often use email spoofing to appear as if they come from trusted organizations such as banks or government agencies.

---

## Prevention Methods

- Email filtering
- Anti-spoofing protocols
- Network monitoring
- Authentication mechanisms
- User awareness training

---

# Conclusion

Network security threats continue to evolve and become more sophisticated. Organizations must implement strong security measures, educate users, and continuously monitor networks to reduce vulnerabilities and protect sensitive data.

Understanding threats such as DoS attacks, MITM attacks, and spoofing is essential for maintaining cybersecurity and ensuring secure communication systems.
